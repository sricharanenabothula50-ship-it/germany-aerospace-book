🚦 Start here: your route in 60 seconds

Three gates decide everything. Check them first.

Gate 1 — Marks ≥ 700/1000 (70%)

Overall Inter total (Y1+Y2), not best-of-5. Below 700 → both Bachelor routes close; finish a full B.Tech, come for a Master.

Gate 2 — MPC stream

Maths + Physics + Chemistry. BiPC/MEC/CEC cannot enter aerospace.

Gate 3 — German C1

Needed for 95% of Bachelors. Only TUM's Aerospace BSc is fully English (+ German A2).

Then pick a road

A · Studienkolleg: Inter → free 1-yr T-Kurs in Germany → FSP exam → Bachelor.
B · Bridge: Inter → 1st-year Mech/Aero B.Tech in India → direct Bachelor Year-1.

JEE truth: only JEE Advanced counts (direct entry). Mains alone and EAMCET count for nothing — never mention EAMCET in your SOP.

📄 APS → VPD → visa, in order

One mistake costs months. Follow the chain.

1 · APS (aps-india.de)
  • Quiz → XII procedure (leave JEE blank) → ₹18,000 via CCAvenue
  • Courier colour A4 both sides + signed letter to Delhi DLTA, R.K. Khanna Stadium, 1 Africa Ave, 110029
  • Digital PDF — never rename/merge it
2 · VPD (my.uni-assist.de)
  • Upload untouched APS PDF + memos + language cert (~€75)
  • 4–6 weeks, valid 1 year for one university
  • Apply yourself on each portal with VPD + SOP/CV
3 · Blocked + insurance
  • After admission: €11,904 + buffer via Expatrio/Fintiba
  • €30k travel insurance + TK/AOK confirmation
4 · CSP + VFS
  • digital.diplo.de pre-check → biometrics at assigned VFS
  • Fee €75 + ~₹3,259 · wait 8–12 wks · start 6–8 mo early
  • Interview: course knowledge, finance math, coherent plan, no fakes

❓ FAQ

Do I need JEE or EAMCET?
No. Only JEE Advanced gives direct entry; Mains alone and EAMCET are unrecognised. Go via Kolleg or the 1-year bridge.
What if I scored below 70%?
Both Bachelor roads close. Finish a full B.Tech in India, then apply for an English Master (Bremen, Darmstadt, FH Aachen — all free).
Telugu medium — am I eligible?
Yes, same roads — but IELTS is mandatory and German C1 is harder. Start both together.
Can I study fully in English?
Bachelor: only TUM (plus German A2). Masters: Bremen, Darmstadt, FH Aachen, Würzburg, BTU — all free. Everything else needs German C1.
How much will my family spend?
One-time ~₹2–2.5L (APS, German, IELTS, exams, visa, flight) + blocked ₹10.5–12.8L (refundable monthly). From semester 2, part-time work covers most living costs.
What if the visa is rejected?
No appeal exists since July 2025. Fix the stated reason (usually funds or language proof) and refile.

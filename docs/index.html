1. The 60-second decision

JEE is NOT compulsory. Only JEE Advanced counts in Germany; Mains alone and EAMCET count for nothing.

  • Three gates: 1) Inter total 700+/1000 (70%) 2) MPC stream 3) German C1 for 95% of Bachelors (only TUM's BSc is fully English).
  • Two roads, both 4 years: A) Studienkolleg: Inter, free 1-year T-Kurs in Germany, FSP exam, Bachelor. B) Bridge: Inter, 1st-year Mech/Aero B.Tech in India, direct Bachelor Year-1.
  • Money 2026: blocked 11,904/yr (refundable monthly) + semester 97-440 + living 850-1500/mo + APS Rs 18k + IELTS Rs 17k + German to B1 about Rs 1.2L. Tuition: 0 except Stuttgart (1,500/sem) and TUM (3,000/sem BSc).
  • Below 700/1000? Both Bachelor roads close. Finish a full B.Tech, come for a Master instead.

Part I - Foundations

*How Germany decides who may study. Read once, then use as reference.*

2. Why Class 12 alone is never enough

Germany's Abitur takes 13 school years; India's 10+2 takes 12. So DAAD's rule (verified on daad.in) is:

  • Indian Class 12 alone = NO direct Bachelor admission, from ANY board (TSBIE, CBSE, ICSE, AP).
  • Exactly three exceptions: JEE Advanced rank (direct, engineering only) / 1 completed Bachelor year in the same field at a recognised university / Studienkolleg + FSP pass.
  • Every university is autonomous: it checks your file itself. APS only verifies documents; uni-assist only forwards them. Neither admits you.

3. The 70% rule (from winter 2026/27)

Announced 23.02.2026, in Anabin since 15.03.2026:

  • You need at least 70% of the maximum achievable overall marks in Class 12, identical for CBSE/ICSE/State boards, for BOTH the Studienkolleg route and the 1-year bridge route.
  • It is the OVERALL percentage, not best-of-5. Grace-marks edge cases: confirm with info@aps-india.de.
  • Below 70%: do a full Indian degree first, then apply for a Master. Do not pay any agent promising otherwise.

4. APS: the mandatory stamp

Since 01.11.2022 every Indian qualification needs APS (aps-india.de, Science Section, German Embassy Delhi). No APS = no uni-assist processing = no visa.

  • Fee Rs 18,000, non-refundable. Pay online via CCAvenue (auto-verified); manual transfers delay you.
  • Steps: eligibility quiz, pick the right procedure (Class XII / JEE / undergrad-2-semesters), register online, print + hand-sign, courier complete colour A4 copies (both sides) to Delhi. Never send originals unless asked.
  • Time: 3-4 weeks normal, 8-10 weeks in May-Aug / Oct-Feb. Apply 4-6 months before your university deadline.
  • Trap: applying after semester 1 and again after semester 2 means paying twice (the assessment category changes). Apply ONCE, when your stage is complete.
  • Result: a digital PDF with e-signature. Never rename, edit or merge it. Exemptions exist only for full degrees earned outside India, IB diplomas, and PhDs with supervisor confirmation.

5. Anabin H+ in 5 minutes

Anabin (anabin.kmk.org) is the German database that says whether your Indian institution counts as a university.

  • Go to Institutionen, search country India, type your university's name (e.g. Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University Hyderabad).
  • H+ = recognised. H+/- = unclear, avoid for the bridge. H- = not recognised (APS may refuse a positive certificate).
  • The status that matters is the degree-awarding university, not your college building. Affiliated colleges inherit it only if that university is H+. Screenshot and print the H+ page for your file.

6. Uni-assist and the VPD

180+ universities outsource document screening to uni-assist (my.uni-assist.de). TUM, RWTH and Stuttgart Aerospace Masters need a VPD if your degree is non-German.

  • Create one online application per study wish, upload PDFs (fully digital), pay (about 75 + 30 per extra wish [UNVERIFIED - check the pay page]), wait 4-6 weeks (Asia: 3-4).
  • You receive a VPD PDF, valid 1 year for one specific university. Uni-assist does NOT forward it: you apply YOURSELF on the university portal before its deadline, attaching the VPD + SOP/CV.
  • Real deadline math: VPD 6-8 weeks BEFORE the university deadline. A 15 July university deadline means your VPD application goes out in late May.

Part II - Every aerospace college

*English options first, then German. One chapter per real choice.*

7. English Bachelor: TUM Aerospace B.Sc.

The ONLY fully English Aerospace Bachelor in Germany. Garching/Ottobrunn near Munich. 6 semesters, 180 ECTS, winter intake only (apply roughly 15.05-15.07).

  • Cost: 3,000/semester tuition (non-EU since WS24/25) + 97 contribution = about 18,582 for 3 years + Munich living (1,200-1,500/mo).
  • Entry: English B2 + German A2 mandatory (Goethe A2 etc.) + aptitude assessment + 8-week pre-internship (2 weeks before end of semester 2 at latest).
  • Still needs: a valid HZB path (Kolleg/1-year/JEE) - English does not waive it. Official: tum.de Aerospace B.Sc. page.

8. English Masters I: Bremen + Darmstadt (FREE)

Uni Bremen Space Engineering M.Sc. - the best free English value. 4 semesters, 100% English, 0 tuition + ~425/sem contribution. Needs C1 English (waived if your last degree was in English), an engineering/physics Bachelor with 24+ CP aero content, an online entrance test and a motivation letter. Local NC, max 30/semester. Apply roughly 15.03-30.04.

TU Darmstadt Aerospace M.Sc. - the TU9 English Master. 4 semesters, 120 ECTS, 0 tuition, English C1. Entry includes a 2-hour online exam over 7 topics (mechanics, thermo, machine elements, control, measurement, numerics, fluids). Winter + summer intake.

9. English Masters II: FH Aachen + Wuerzburg + BTU (FREE)

FH Aachen Aerospace M.Sc. - 3 semesters, English (1-2 modules German), 0 tuition. Needs a 7-semester/210 ECTS relevant Bachelor + IELTS 6.5/TOEFL 92 + GRE quantitative 83rd percentile for non-EHEA degrees. Non-EU deadlines 31.05 (winter) / 30.11 (summer).

JMU Wuerzburg Aerospace Informatics / Satellite Tech Elite - 4 semesters, English from B2 level (IELTS 6.0/TOEFL 72), 0 tuition, plus German A2 by end of semester 2. Built on real pico-satellite projects (13+ own satellites in orbit).

BTU Cottbus Transfers-Fluids-Materials - joint degree Bordeaux/Louvain/Cottbus, 4 semesters with mobility, English IELTS 6.0, 0 tuition + mobility costs. Scholarship deadline January, self-funded March.

10. Expensive English Masters (backups only)

TUM Aerospace M.Sc. / ESPACE - 6,000/semester (about 24,388 for 2 years), IELTS 6.5/TOEFL 88, aptitude assessment. Pay for the brand and Munich industry.

TU Berlin MSE Space Engineering - continuing-education Master, 24,900 total + organisation fee, needs 1 year of practical experience. A backup, not a first choice.

11. German TUs: the big five

All need German C1 (DSH-2/TestDaF 4x4) and a Kolleg/1-year/JEE path. All are 0 tuition except Stuttgart.

  • RWTH Aachen LRT M.Sc. - 3 semesters, 90 ECTS, German with some English. Needs 145 CP engineering background, 20-week internship, GRE for non-EU applicants [verify currency]. No extra non-EU tuition.
  • Uni Stuttgart LRT B.Sc./M.Sc. - the classic. B.Sc. has NC (selection via DoSV); 1,500/semester non-EU tuition (Baden-Wuerttemberg law) + 184 contribution.
  • TU Berlin LRT M.Sc. - open admission + subject fitness, winter/summer, ~379 contribution.
  • TU Braunschweig LRT M.Sc. - open admission, sits at the research airport with DLR next door.
  • TU Hamburg (TUHH) Aviation M.Sc. - open admission, German B2.2 at application rising to C1 at enrolment.
  • TU Dresden - keeps the traditional 10-semester Diplom in Mechanical with aerospace specialisation + newer Master structures; check SINS for exact rules.

12. Fachhochschulen: the practical road

Small cohorts (~40:1 vs 200:1 at TUs), ex-industry professors, a MANDATORY 20-22-week industry semester built into the degree (it does not eat your 140-day work quota), and a direct pipeline to Airbus/Lufthansa Technik/MTU/Diehl. An FH Master is legally equal for industry and the Blue Card; only a direct PhD needs a cooperating TU supervisor.

  • HAW Hamburg Aircraft Engineering - 7 semesters incl. 22-week internship, 397/sem incl. Deutschland ticket, German C1 + English B1. Sits next to the Airbus A320 final assembly line.
  • HS Bremen - ILST Bachelor (German) + Aerospace Technologies Master (sem 1 German C1, sem 2-3 English) + fee-based Aeronautical Management M.Eng in English (9,800 total + 1 year aviation experience).
  • HM Munich / THI Ingolstadt - solid German-taught Bavarian options near MTU/Airbus Defence (note Munich non-EU fees at HM: 500/700 per semester).
  • TH Wildau AVIMA - English aviation-management Master (11-12.5k total + 1 year experience), management not engineering.
  • DHBW Friedrichshafen (dual) - paid study with Airbus DS: you need the company contract 12-18 months BEFORE enrolment, interviews mostly in German B2-C1. Without that contract it is not an option from India.

13. What to exclude (and why)

  • UniBw Munich Aerospace - officers/soldiers only, German only. No Indian self-funded route.
  • Private universities (Constructor/IU/SRH/GISMA/Lancaster) - NO pure aerospace degree; closest are Robotics/Mechatronics at 10-27k/year. Never pay that for a non-aerospace degree.
  • TU Delft - Netherlands, different country and system. TUM Asia Singapore - not Germany.
  • Worms/Frankfurt Aviation Management, Wildau AVIMA - business degrees, not engineering; they do not qualify you as an aerospace engineer.
  • Lilium (insolvent Oct 2024, ceased Dec 2024) - do not plan your career around it. Volocopter - near-zero hiring. ESA staff posts - require ESA-member citizenship; Indians enter via contractors or DLR.

Part III - Money

*Budgets a parent can verify line by line.*

14. Tuition and semester fees

Federal default: 0 tuition, only the semester contribution (student services + student body + Deutschland semester ticket).

  • TUM: Bachelor 2,000/3,000 per program, Master 4,000/6,000 (Aerospace BSc 3,000; MSc/ESPACE 6,000) + 97. Fixed for standard duration + 1 semester per cohort; waivers 500-1,800/semester exist.
  • Baden-Wuerttemberg (Stuttgart/KIT/HKA/DHBW): 1,500/semester non-EU + ~184 contribution. Exempt: EU/EEA, German Abitur holders, settlement-permit holders, refugees.
  • Examples WS25/26: RWTH ~298, TU Berlin 379.06 (50 admin + 14.56 student + 85 Werk + 226.80 ticket + 2.70 fund), Braunschweig 440, Hamburg 384, HAW 397, Bremen ~380-425.
  • Second degrees in BW 650/semester; long-term fees in Lower Saxony 500/semester after standard duration + 6.

15. Blocked account 2026

Student visa: 11,904/year = 992/month, unchanged since Sep 2024 (no 2026 rise announced). Chancenkarte/job-seeker: 13,092 (1,091/month). Always transfer 100-150 extra as buffer (about 12,050).

  • Expatrio: 119 setup + 9/month (tariff since 07.07.26). Fintiba: Basic 89 + 4.90/month, Plus 277.80 incl. insurance (fastest confirmation for tight appointments). Deutsche Bank: 150 by post, 4-8 weeks, too slow unless early. Coracle: stopped new accounts in 2025 - walk away if any agent offers it.
  • Money must come from you or close family (friends/business transfers get rejected). It covers year 1 only. Open it AFTER admission (refunds take 4-8 weeks) but 8-12 weeks before the visa appointment.

16. Living costs by city

DAAD baseline: 900-1,200/month needed; visa minimum 992 is NOT Munich-realistic.

  • Munich 1,200-1,500 (dorm avg 400.60 with 1-7 semester waitlist; WG 550-900; plan 1,500).
  • Stuttgart 1,000-1,300 (dorm 280-505). Berlin 1,000-1,300 (WG 400-650). Hamburg 950-1,250. Aachen 850-1,000 (WG 250-450, most affordable aero cluster). Braunschweig/Bremen 850-1,050.
  • Groceries 200-300 (Aldi/Lidl), Mensa meals 2.50-5, dorm vs WG makes the biggest difference. Apply for Studierendenwerk housing the day you get admission.

17. Health insurance + tickets

Under 30 and enrolled = public insurance compulsory (TK/AOK/Barmer). 2026 TK: 141.16 (<23 or with child) / 146.29 (23+ childless) per month = 110.38 health incl. 2.69% extra + 30.78/35.91 care. AOK same; Barmer/DAK slightly higher. Over 30 or 14+ semesters: voluntary ~220 + 47-55. Private over-30 tariffs from ~79-129 (under 45).

  • Deutschland ticket: 63/month from 01.01.2026 (was 49, then 58). Semester ticket about 208.80-226.80/semester; TUM's Bavarian ticket is separate from the 97. Broadcast fee (Rundfunkbeitrag): 18.36/month per flat, split in a WG.

18. Taxes on student jobs

Minijob 2026: 603/month (7,236/year) at 13.90/hour minimum wage (14.60 already locked for 2027). Midijob range 603.01-2,000 with sliding contributions. A minijob is free of health/care/unemployment insurance; pension is compulsory (3.6% yourself unless exempted).

  • Werkstudent privilege: max 20h/week during lectures, unlimited in holidays inside the day-cap, max 26 weeks/182 days over 20h per rolling year; only pension is deducted. Lose the 20h discipline and you lose student insurance status.
  • Single with no kids = tax class I automatically; second job = class VI (highest). Basic tax-free allowance 12,348 (2026); withholding above it is refunded by filing (4 years retroactive). Solidarity surcharge is ~0 for students. Deduct semester fees, books and travel.

Part IV - The Telangana playbook

*TSBIE specifics, then the two roads in full.*

19. TSBIE decoded

Telangana State Board of Intermediate Education, Nampally Hyderabad (tsbie.cgg.gov.in - portal address migrates, check both TSBIE/TGBIE spellings). Inter 1st year (Class 11) 500 + 2nd year (Class 12) 500 = 1000 total. Only MPC (Maths/Physics/Chemistry) qualifies for aerospace; BiPC/MEC/CEC do not.

  • Subjects: English + second language + Maths IA/IB + IIA/IIB + Physics + Chemistry + practicals. Use the printed maximum on YOUR long memo for the percentage, never memory.
  • Documents you will collect: Y1 short memo + Y2 short memo + LONG consolidated memo (via college, months later) + pass certificate + TC from the principal + study/conduct certificate + SSC memo + English-medium certificate. 10+ colour copies + 300 dpi uncropped PDF scans, no passwords.

20. Road A: Studienkolleg, step by step

For students who can reach German B1 within ~9 months of Inter and move to Germany within a year.

1. Inter result (Apr/May): compute %, chase long memo + pass certificate (TS colleges delay to Aug-Sep).

2. German A1 to B1 intensive + IELTS 6.5 in parallel.

3. APS XII-procedure (leave JEE blank), pre-alerting principal + TSBIE.

4. TestAS Core + Engineering (target 110+) + VPD via uni-assist.

5. Apply to 3-5 public Kollegs (15.07 / 15.01) - T-Kurs for university aero, TI-Kurs for FH.

6. Aufnahmepruefung in Germany (invitation = visa basis) - German onSET + class-11 Maths.

7. Kolleg, 2 semesters, ~32 hrs/week, free + ~300/semester.

8. FSP in June/July (German 4.5h + Maths 180 min + Physics 180 min + oral).

9. Bachelor application with FSP + C1 - start October.

  • English Kolleg alternatives (rare, fee-based): FH Aachen Freshman, Constructor Foundation (~12-13k/year).

21. Road B: the 1-year bridge, step by step

For students whose German is zero now, who missed Kolleg deadlines, or who want an Indian backup.

1. After Inter (70%+), join 1st-year B.Tech Mechanical/Aerospace/Mechatronics/Electrical at an H+ university: NIT-W, JNTUH-CEH, OUCE, then CBIT/Vasavi/MGIT if JNTUH/OU-awarded. Check anabin yourself; never trust a brochure.

2. Finish BOTH semesters, no backlogs, aim 8.0+ CGPA. Mech-to-Aero is accepted; CSE-to-Aero is NOT (wrong field - rejection or Kolleg anyway). Never take CSE as an easy-CGPA shortcut.

3. Learn German A1 to B2 + IELTS 6.5 during that year; keep stamped syllabus copies for the ECTS audit.

4. ONE APS undergrad-2-semesters application AFTER year 1, then VPD, then direct Bachelor Year-1. No Kolleg.

  • Cost logic: government bridge (~Rs 1-1.5L) saves a ~11-12k EUR Kolleg living year. Costly private bridges (BITS/VIT-level fees) erase the saving.

22. Hyderabad practical guide

German: Goethe-Zentrum Hyderabad + TestDaF/telc planning 3-4 months ahead (May-Aug fills first). IELTS: IDP/BC centres (Begumpet, Kukatpally, Dilsukhnagar), Academic centre-based, never home editions. VFS Hyderabad (Punjagutta Metro) serves the Chennai German mission covering Telangana - always follow the CSP letter, never book Delhi/Mumbai links. Blocked funding via SBI/HDFC/ICICI LRS (A2 form, PAN, admission letter) from self/parents only. Memo delays: Narayana/Chaitanya long memos + pass certificates often slip to Aug-Sep - APS needs the LONG memo, so chase from June. Name initials: align passport + APS + uni-assist exactly via affidavit BEFORE APS. EAMCET rank: useless for Germany, never mention it in the SOP.

Part V - Language exams

*What to book, in what order, at what cost.*

23. German levels and costs

Degree taught in German needs C1: TestDaF 4x4 / DSH-2 (Germany only) / Goethe C1 / telc C1 Hochschule / OESD C1. Kolleg application needs B1 on paper; passing the entrance realistically needs B2.

  • Hyderabad prices: A1/A2/B1 Rs 27,730 per level at Goethe; exams A1 9,400 / A2 10,600 / B1 18,800 (or 4,700 per module retake) / B2 21,200 / C1 24,000. Total A1 to B1 about Rs 83,190 + 38,800 exams.
  • Zero to B1: 6-9 months intensive (620-1,100 hours with self-study). Zero to C1: 12-18 months, 800-1,000+ hours. Private institutes are cheaper but the VISA needs a Goethe/telc/TestDaF centre certificate - a private internal certificate is worthless at the mission.
  • Never book TestDaF below solid B2: it is one single exam with no module retakes (unlike Goethe/telc).

24. TestAS and IELTS

TestAS (testas.de): Core module (quantitative/logical, 110 min paper) + Engineering subject module (45 tasks/145 min) for Aerospace. Choose the module at registration - it locks. English or German, about 4.5 hours. Standard scores 70-130 (mean 100): target 110+ competitive, 115+ for TUM/RWTH/KIT or to compensate a CGPA under 7. Dates: 24.10.26 / 26.11.26 / 25.02.27; book 8-10 weeks ahead. JEE holders may be exempt at some universities - confirm each.

  • IELTS Academic 6.0-7.0 / TOEFL iBT 80-100 is the published band (TUM 6.5/88, Bremen C1 ~7.0/95, Wuerzburg/BTU 6.0/72-79). Book centre-based Academic (~Rs 17k); keep the certificate under 1 year old for the visa unless the admission letter confirms your level. MOI letters sometimes unlock ADMISSION (Bremen accepts last-degree-in-English; Constructor accepts 6-year English) but almost never the VISA.

Part VI - The visa chain

*Paperwork in exact order. One mistake costs months.*

25. Blocked, insurance, uni-assist timing

Open the blocked account AFTER admission (refunds take 4-8 weeks on failure) but 8-12 weeks before the visa slot. Add travel/incoming insurance (30k Schengen minimum for the gap) plus the public insurer confirmation (TK/AOK/Barmer ~140-160/month from semester start) needed for enrolment.

  • Uni-assist document rules (India): Class 12 certificate in original language (+ sworn translation only if not English/German), the APS PDF uploaded unmodified (it is verifiable online), full transcripts with the grading-scale page, language certificate, passport page, plus program extras (CV/SOP/LOR/internships). Scans: complete, uncropped, legible at 100%, PDF, no password.

26. CSP, VFS and the interview

Since Jan 2025 everything runs via the Consular Services Portal (digital.diplo.de): upload the 10-slot Study-APS file (passport, Class 12, APS, admission, signed motivation, tabular CV, language, finance, insurance, degrees/VPD), pass the pre-check, then book biometrics ONLY via the CSP link. Fee 75 EUR (~Rs 8,500) + VFS ~Rs 2,500. Processing is officially 6-8 weeks, realistically 8-12 in India at peak (Chennai/Bengaluru have hit 8-12-week waits). Start 6-8 months before classes.

  • The 5-15 minute interview checks four things: genuine student, researched course (name modules + a lab), solid finance (blocked + sponsor + monthly payout math), coherent plan. Explain a gap year with certificates (German + TestAS + APS dates); identical dates across CV/SOP/CSP. Fake documents mean a ban plus a report to German authorities.

27. SOP, CV and LOR that work

SOP 500-800 words (some programs cap at 1 page or 1,000-3,000 characters - the program page binds, and TUM runs plagiarism checks): fit + academic readiness + career rationale. Formal, factual, no hardship narrative, no CV repetition, no copied web text; name 3-4 modules plus professors/labs. Uni-assist does NOT evaluate it - it goes to the university WITH the VPD. The visa motivation letter is separate (why Germany + program + finance + post-plan, signed, consistent with the CV).

  • LORs: 2-3 academic (professors/supervisors), letterhead + signature, specific examples + rank/context + research aptitude. Request in October for summer intake. CV: tabular 1-2 pages, 11-12 pt, reverse chronological, uniform MM/YYYY, education first for recent graduates, 3-5 relevant courses with outcomes, tools, languages with CEFR + scores + dates. No Canva graphics, star bars or photos unless asked.

Part VII - After the degree

*Scholarships first, then work, residence and employers.*

28. Scholarships that fit Indians

DAAD EPOS (Bachelor + 2 years experience, DAC list incl. India, 992/month Master, 1,300 PhD + travel + 460/year + insurance, apply direct to the listed course, mostly Aug-Nov). DAAD WISE (IIT/NIT/IISER/BITS 5th-6th sem, find the German professor yourself, 2-3 months May-Aug, ~692-750/month + 1,200 travel). Deutschlandstipendium 300/month (150 federal + 150 private sponsor, all nationalities, no income test, no repayment, awarded by the university itself - TUM ~800/year). Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters (study in 2+ EU countries, 1,400/month up to 24 months + tuition; India is the world's #1 recipient). Party foundations (Boell/KAS/Ebert/Luxemburg: ~992/month + extras, need B2 German + civic engagement + 1 Sept / 1 Mar / 15 July deadlines). Company/aero: DLR-DAAD fellowships (thesis 9 mo 992; doctoral 36 mo 1,760; postdoc 2,400), Munich Aerospace PhD (1,575/month, 3 years), TUM international one-off 500-1,800/semester.

29. Work rules 2024-2026

Permits issued after 1 March 2024: 140 full / 280 half days per calendar year (was 120/240). Full = over 4 hours, half = up to 4 hours. Alternative counting: 20h/week = 2.5 days; the mission applies whichever is more favourable weekly. HiWi jobs at the university/Studierendenwerk/AStA + compulsory internships are UNLIMITED and uncounted; voluntary internships count; freelancing needs permission.

  • The 20h lecture-period rule (social law): stay at or under 20h to keep the Werkstudent privilege (no health/care/unemployment deductions, pension only). Over 20h is allowed on weekends/evenings/nights up to 26 weeks/182 days per year, and full-time in semester breaks inside the 140-day cap. Minimum wage: 12.82 (2025), 13.90 (2026), 14.60 (2027 locked). 20h x 13.90 = about 1,204/month gross.

30. Post-study to Blue Card to PR

Graduation gives an 18-month one-off non-extendable job-seeker permit (any gainful work allowed while you search for a qualified job; apply before the student permit expires; >6 months abroad kills it without prior permission).

  • EU Blue Card thresholds: 2025: 48,300 regular / 43,759.80 shortage+new-grads; 2026: 50,700 / 45,934.20 (BMI announcement, federal gazette 31 Dec). MINT engineers ARE shortage occupations. Needs a recognised degree (Anabin H+ / ZAB), a 6-month+ matching contract; regular needs no agency approval. Max 4 years (contract + 3 months); job changes in the first 12 months need approval.
  • Settlement: general 5 years (study counts half) + 60 pension months + B1; skilled 4 years; Blue Card 21 months with B1 / 27 with A1 (reduced from 33 in March 2024 - many sites still print 33). Citizenship reform of 27 June 2024: 5 years (was 8) + B1 + self-support + test; 3-year exceptional track exists but the 2025 coalition plans to abolish it - check BMI. Germany now allows dual citizenship in principle, but INDIA does not: acquiring German means losing Indian (OCI possible).

31. Employers and the clearance wall

Civil Airbus Commercial (Hamburg-Finkenwerder A320 line, Bremen wings), MTU civil engines (Munich/Hannover), civil DLR institutes, Isar Aerospace Ottobrunn (English-first NewSpace, 50+ openings, visa sponsorship possible for scarce skills), OHB Bremen (200+ openings incl. working students), Diehl Aviation, consultancies like ALTEN (common Indian entry point) - all open. Typical Werkstudent pay 15-18/hour; traditional OEMs want B1-B2 German + CATIA/MATLAB/Python; combine internship + thesis (Airbus allows it); ~75% of offers come via a prior internship.

  • The wall: Airbus Defence sites (Manching, Donauwoerth military), MTU military engines, Hensoldt, Diehl Defence, defence-adjacent DLR and secure OHB satcom frequently advertise EU-citizen/NATO-only (export control/ITAR/EAR, US taint, security vetting UES S1-S3). Strategy: target civil first, disclose nationality early, ask whether export-control vetting applies, build German + a clean internship record, convert after settlement.

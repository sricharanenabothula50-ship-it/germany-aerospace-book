🧭 Start here — your 60-second decision

No JEE? No problem. EAMCET and JEE Mains alone mean nothing in Germany — only JEE Advanced gives direct entry. Without it you have two legal routes, both taking 4 years total:

A · Studienkolleg — Inter → 1-year T-Kurs in Germany (free) → FSP exam → Bachelor. Pick if you can hit German B1 within ~9 months.
B · 1-yr B.Tech bridge — Inter → 1st-year Mech/Aero in India → direct Bachelor Year-1. Pick if your German is zero now.

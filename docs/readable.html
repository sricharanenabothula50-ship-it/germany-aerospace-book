0 · TL;DR — read this first (2 minutes)

JEE is NOT compulsory. About 90% of Telangana students go without it. TG EAPCET (EAMCET) and JEE Mains alone are not recognised in Germany — only JEE Advanced gives direct entry. Without Advanced you have exactly two legal routes:

RouteWhatTotal timeBest if
A · StudienkollegInter → 1-yr T-Kurs in Germany (free) → FSP exam → Bachelor (3 yrs)4 yrsYou can reach German B1 within ~9 months after Inter
B · 1-yr B.Tech bridgeInter → 1st-year Mech/Aero B.Tech in India → direct Bachelor Year-1, no Kolleg1 + 3 = 4 yrsYour German is zero now; you learn during the B.Tech year
Three hard gates (no exceptions): ① Inter total ≥ 700/1000 (70%) — below that both routes close, finish a full B.Tech and come for a Master instead. ② Stream must be MPC. ③ Almost all Bachelors need German C1 (only TUM's Aerospace BSc is fully English + German A2).
Money reality 2026: blocked account €11,904/yr (€992/mo) + semester fee €97–440 + living €850–1,500/mo depending on city + APS ₹18,000 + IELTS ~₹17,000 + German courses ~₹83,000 to B1. Public tuition is €0 except Baden-Württemberg (€1,500/sem: Stuttgart) and TUM (€3,000/sem BSc, €6,000/sem MSc).

3 · Taxes — student jobs

Minijob: €603/mo tax-free-ish (2026, at €13.90/h minimum wage; €14.60 in 2027). Werkstudent: max 20h/week during lectures (unlimited in holidays within the 140 full / 280 half days per year cap); you pay only pension (~9–10%), no health/care/unemployment. Typical aero pay €15–22/h from semester 2 — covers 60–80% of living costs. Income tax starts above €12,348/yr; students effectively pay ~€0 solidarity surcharge. File a tax return — semester fees and books are deductible.

4 · Telangana Board specifics

Board: TSBIE, Nampally Hyderabad (tsbie.cgg.gov.in). Inter 1st year (500) + 2nd year (500) = 1000 total. Percentage = (Y1+Y2)/1000 — overall, not best-of-5.

Collect now: Y1 short memo + Y2 short memo + long consolidated memo (via college, months later — APS needs this, internet printout alone is not enough) + pass certificate + TC from principal + study certificate + SSC memo. Keep name spelling identical to passport (fix initials like "K. Sai Teja" via affidavit before APS). English-medium memos need no translation.

5 · The two no-JEE routes, step by step

Route A — Studienkolleg (leave after Inter)

  1. Inter result (Apr/May) → confirm ≥70%, chase long memo + pass cert (TS colleges delay till Aug–Sep).
  2. German A1→B1 intensive in Hyderabad (6–9 months) + IELTS 6.5 parallel.
  3. APS XII procedure (₹18,000, courier to Delhi, 3–10 weeks) — do NOT use the JEE checklist; leave JEE fields blank. Pre-alert your college principal + TSBIE or verification stalls.
  4. TestAS (Core + Engineering module, target 110+, 115+ for TUM/RWTH) + VPD via uni-assist (4–6 weeks, valid 1 yr — apply 6–8 weeks before the uni deadline).
  5. Apply to 3–5 public Kollegs (deadlines 15.07 / 15.01) → Aufnahmeprüfung in Germany (German + Maths) → Kolleg 2 semesters, free + ~€300/sem.
  6. FSP (June/July) → Bachelor application with FSP + German C1 → start October.

Route B — 1-year B.Tech bridge (stay one year)

  1. After Inter, join 1st-year Mechanical / Aerospace / Mechatronics (NOT CSE) at an H+ university — priority: NIT-W > JNTUH-CEH > OUCE > CBIT/Vasavi/MGIT (if JNTUH/OU-awarded). Verify at anabin.kmk.org → Institutionen → Indien — the degree-awarding university must show H+.
  2. Finish both semesters, no backlogs, aim 8.0+ CGPA; learn German A1→B2 + IELTS alongside; keep stamped syllabus copies.
  3. Single APS undergrad-2-semesters application after year 1 (applying after sem 1 means paying twice) → VPD → direct Bachelor Year-1. No Kolleg.
Rule of thumb: pick A if you can hit B1 by December; pick B if your German is zero and you missed Kolleg deadlines — it also gives you an Indian backup if the visa fails.

7 · Studienkolleg + FSP (KIT-verified pattern)

T-Kurs = for university engineering (German, Maths, Physics, Chemistry, ~32 hrs/week, ~25 students, attendance compulsory). TI-Kurs = FH/informatics-oriented. Public Kollegs (TU Berlin, KIT Karlsruhe, Frankfurt, Hannover, Hamburg, Munich, Nordhausen, Leipzig…) are tuition-free. Aufnahmeprüfung: onSET German (8 gap-texts × 20, 40 min) + digital Maths (up to German class-11 level, no integrals). FSP: German 4.5h (250-word graph essay + listening + reading + grammar) + Maths 180 min + Physics/Chemistry 180 min + oral 15–20 min. Internal FSP is recognised nationwide for restricted engineering entry. Private Kollegs (€5.5–8k) only as backup.

8 · The bridge in one paragraph

Mech→Aero is accepted as "subject-related"; CSE→Aero is not (you'd be rejected as off-field or sent back to Kolleg). So never take CSE as an "easy CGPA" shortcut if Germany-Aero is the goal. Government colleges (JNTUH/OUCE ~₹1–1.5L/yr) make the bridge a net saver versus a Kolleg living year in Germany (~€11–12k); expensive private bridges erase the saving.

9 · APS + VPD + blocked + visa (Hyderabad)

  1. APS (aps-india.de): quiz → register → pay ₹18,000 via CCAvenue → courier colour A4 copies (both sides) + signed authorisation letter to Delhi (Gate 3, DLTA Complex, R.K. Khanna Stadium, 1 Africa Avenue, 110029 — no drop-offs). Track in portal; digital PDF certificate — never rename/merge it.
  2. VPD (my.uni-assist.de): upload unmodified APS PDF + memos + language cert + passport; ~€75 + €30/extra uni; 4–6 weeks. Then apply directly to each university portal with the VPD.
  3. Blocked + insurance: open after admission (€11,904 + buffer via Expatrio/Fintiba) + travel insurance €30k + public insurer (TK/AOK) confirmation.
  4. CSP + VFS: apply on digital.diplo.de first (pre-check), then biometrics at the assigned VFS — Telangana belongs to the Chennai mission (VFS Hyderabad, Punjagutta Metro). Fee €75 + ~₹3,259 VFS. Processing 8–12 weeks at peak — start 6–8 months before departure. Rejections can no longer be remonstrated (since July 2025): you reapply.

10 · SOP, CV, interview, landing

SOP (500–800 words): who you are + which Kolleg/program → academic fit (MPC marks, drone/rocket project, Python/MATLAB/CAD, TestAS) → why this Kolleg/uni (name 2–3 modules + a lab — show you read the module handbook) → honest no-JEE line (no Advanced rank, so T-Kurs+FSP is the recognised path; never trash-talk India; don't lead with "free tuition") → future (FSP → named Bachelor → labs/internship). CV: tabular, 1–2 pages, reverse-chronological, no gaps since Inter, identical spellings everywhere. LORs ×2: Inter Maths + Physics lecturers, letterhead, rank + two specific examples. Interview (5–15 min): prove you're genuine — know your city, semester fee, housing plan, course duration (Kolleg 2 sem + Bachelor 6 sem); explain the gap year with certificates (German + TestAS + APS); finances (blocked + sponsor). After landing: housing confirmation → Anmeldung within 14 days → bank activation (€992/mo starts) → enrolment → residence permit (~€100, eAT card).

11 · Scholarships, work, Blue Card, PR, employers

Scholarships: Deutschlandstipendium €300/mo (all nationalities) · DAAD EPOS €992/mo (needs 2 yrs experience) · WISE research internships (IIT/NIT) · Erasmus Mundus €1,400/mo. Work: 140 full / 280 half days per year; HiWi + compulsory internships unlimited. After graduation: 18-month job-seeker visa → EU Blue Card 2026: €50,700 (shortage/MINT: €45,934 — every aero starting salary of €48–75k clears it: Airbus €58–68k, MTU/Rolls €60–72k, DLR ~€52–60k) → settlement in 21 months with B1 (27 with A1) → citizenship after 5 years (note: India bans dual citizenship — you'd take OCI). Clearance warning: military/secret programs (Eurofighter, A400M-military, FCAS) routinely require EU/NATO citizenship — target civil programs first (A320, civil MRO, civil DLR/OHB/Isar), disclose nationality early.

Preface

The 60-second decision

Read only this page if you are in a hurry.

  • JEE is NOT compulsory. 90% of Telangana students go without it. Only JEE Advanced counts in Germany; Mains alone and EAMCET count for nothing.
  • Three gates, no exceptions: ① Inter total ≥ 700/1000 (70%) ② stream MPC ③ German C1 for 95% of Bachelors (only TUM's BSc is fully English).
  • Two roads, both 4 years: A — Studienkolleg: Inter → free 1-year T-Kurs in Germany → FSP exam → Bachelor. B — Bridge: Inter → 1st-year Mech/Aero B.Tech in India → direct Bachelor Year-1.
  • Money 2026: blocked €11,904/yr (refundable monthly) + semester €97–440 + living €850–1,500/mo + APS ₹18k + IELTS ₹17k + German to B1 ≈ ₹1.2L. Tuition itself: €0 except Stuttgart (€1,500/sem) and TUM (€3,000/sem BSc).
  • Below 700/1000? Both Bachelor roads close — finish a full B.Tech, come for a (mostly English) Master instead.

Chapter II

Money: fees, living, health, taxes

Every euro, point by point.

  • Tuition: public = €0 + semester contribution €97–440 (TUM €97 · Stuttgart €184 · Berlin €379 · Braunschweig €440 · HAW €397).
  • Exceptions: Baden-Württemberg €1,500/sem (Stuttgart/KIT/HKA) · TUM BSc €3,000 / MSc €6,000 · second degree €650.
  • Blocked account (visa proof, paid back monthly): €11,904/yr = €992/mo + €100–150 buffer. Expatrio (€119+€9/mo) or Fintiba (€159+€9.90/mo). Coracle is dead since 2025 — never use it.
  • Living/month: Munich €1,200–1,500 · Stuttgart/Berlin €1,000–1,300 · Hamburg €950–1,250 · Aachen/Bremen/Braunschweig €850–1,050.
  • Health (TK 2026): €141.16/mo (under 23) · €146.29/mo (23+, childless). Transport: Deutschland ticket €63/mo · semester ticket €208–227 · Rundfunk €18.36/mo per flat.
  • Student jobs: minijob €603/mo · Werkstudent max 20h/week in lectures (unlimited in holidays within 140 full / 280 half days a year) at €15–22/h from semester 2 — covers 60–80% of living costs. Income tax starts above €12,348/yr; as a student you effectively pay ~€0.
Chapter III

Your Telangana board, decoded

TSBIE, Nampally Hyderabad — the exact math that decides everything.

  • Total = 1000: Inter 1st year (500) + 2nd year (500). Percentage = (Y1+Y2)/1000 — overall, never best-of-5.
  • 850 / 750 → eligible for both roads. 700 → borderline eligible. 680 or less → both Bachelor roads closed.
  • MPC only. BiPC/MEC/CEC cannot enter T-Kurs Aerospace.
  • Collect now: Y1 short memo + Y2 short memo + long consolidated memo (comes via college months later — APS needs this; the internet printout alone fails) + pass certificate + TC from principal + study certificate + SSC memo.
  • Name = passport, letter for letter. Fix initials ("K. Sai Teja") by affidavit before APS. English-medium memos need no translation.
Chapter IV

The two roads without JEE

Pick one. Both take four years.

Road A — Studienkolleg (leave after Inter):

  • Confirm ≥70% → chase long memo + pass certificate (TS colleges delay till Aug–Sep).
  • German A1→B1 intensive in Hyderabad (6–9 months) + IELTS 6.5 alongside.
  • APS XII procedure (₹18,000, Delhi, 3–10 weeks) — leave JEE fields blank; warn your principal + TSBIE or verification stalls.
  • TestAS Core+Engineering (target 110+, 115+ for TUM/RWTH) + VPD via uni-assist (4–6 weeks, valid 1 year).
  • Apply to 3–5 public Kollegs (deadlines 15.07 / 15.01) → Aufnahmeprüfung in Germany → free T-Kurs, 2 semesters → FSP (June/July) → Bachelor from October.

Road B — 1-year B.Tech bridge (stay one year):

  • Join 1st-year Mechanical / Aerospace / Mechatronics — never CSE — at an H+ university. Priority: NIT-W › JNTUH-CEH › OUCE › CBIT/Vasavi/MGIT (if JNTUH/OU-awarded). Verify the degree-awarding university at anabin.kmk.org.
  • Finish both semesters, zero backlogs, aim 8.0+ CGPA; learn German to B2 + IELTS; keep stamped syllabus copies.
  • One single APS undergrad-2-semesters application after year 1 (applying after sem 1 means paying twice) → VPD → direct Bachelor Year-1. No Kolleg.
  • Rule: pick A if B1 by December is realistic; pick B if German is zero now — it doubles as an Indian backup if the visa fails.
Chapter V

German, TestAS & IELTS in Hyderabad

  • Goethe-Zentrum, Banjara Hills (20 Journalists Colony Rd 3): Rs 27,730 per level. Exams — A1 ₹9,400 · A2 ₹10,600 · B1 ₹18,800 · B2 ₹21,200 · C1 ₹24,000. Zero→B1 ≈ ₹1.2L and 6–9 months intensive; →C1 12–18 months.
  • TestDaF €195 — no fixed Hyderabad slot; travel to Chennai/Bengaluru/Delhi/Pune. telc via German Drishty Hyderabad. Degree needs C1 (TestDaF 4×4 / DSH-2 / Goethe C1 / telc C1 Hochschule); Kolleg application B1 on paper, B2 in reality.
  • TestAS: 24 Oct / 26 Nov 2026 / 25 Feb 2027 — Core + Engineering, in English or German.
  • IELTS: IDP Begumpet/Kukatpally, Academic ₹17,000 (paper) / ₹19,000 (computer) — centre-based only.
  • Visa rejects: Duolingo, PTE, TOEFL Home Edition and MOI letters — confirmed by the German missions in India. Always book a centre test.
Chapter VI

Studienkolleg & FSP, exam by exam

  • T-Kurs = university engineering (German, Maths, Physics, Chemistry, ~32 hrs/week, ~25 students, attendance compulsory). TI-Kurs = FH/informatics track. Public Kollegs (Berlin, KIT Karlsruhe, Frankfurt, Hannover, Hamburg, Munich, Nordhausen…) are tuition-free.
  • Aufnahmeprüfung: onSET German (8 gap-texts × 20 blanks, 40 min) + digital Maths (German class-11 level, no integrals).
  • FSP: German 4.5h (250-word graph essay + listening + reading + grammar) · Maths 180 min (non-programmable calculator + formula sheet) · Physics/Chemistry 180 min · oral 15–20 min. An internal FSP is recognised nationwide.
  • Private Kollegs (€5.5–8k) only as backup if you fail twice or miss the deadline.
Chapter VII

The 1-year bridge, in one page

  • Mech → Aero is accepted as "subject-related". CSE → Aero is not — you would be rejected or sent back to Kolleg. Never take CSE as an "easy CGPA" shortcut.
  • Government colleges (JNTUH/OUCE ≈ ₹1–1.5L/yr) make the bridge a net saver against a Kolleg living year in Germany (~€11–12k); expensive private bridges erase the saving.
  • Collect: both sem memos + promotion proof + stamped syllabus (for the German ECTS audit) + English-medium letter.
Chapter VIII

APS → VPD → blocked → visa

  • APS (aps-india.de): quiz → register → pay ₹18,000 via CCAvenue → courier colour A4 copies (both sides) + signed authorisation letter to Delhi (Gate 3, DLTA Complex, R.K. Khanna Stadium, 1 Africa Avenue, 110029 — no drop-offs). Track online; the digital PDF certificate must never be renamed or merged.
  • VPD (my.uni-assist.de): upload the untouched APS PDF + memos + language cert + passport (~€75 + €30/extra uni, 4–6 weeks). Then apply directly on each university portal with the VPD.
  • Blocked + insurance only after admission (€11,904 + buffer via Expatrio/Fintiba) + €30k travel insurance + public insurer (TK/AOK) confirmation.
  • CSP + VFS: apply on digital.diplo.de first (pre-check), then biometrics at the assigned VFS — Telangana belongs to the Chennai mission (VFS Hyderabad, Punjagutta Metro). Fee €75 + ~₹3,259. Processing 8–12 weeks at peak — start 6–8 months before flying. Rejections can no longer be appealed (since July 2025): you reapply.
Chapter IX

SOP, CV, interview & landing

  • SOP (500–800 words): who you are + which Kolleg/program → fit (MPC marks, drone/rocket project, Python/MATLAB/CAD, TestAS) → why this place (name 2–3 modules + a lab — prove you read the handbook) → honest no-JEE line (no Advanced rank, so T-Kurs+FSP is the recognised path; never criticise India; don't lead with "free tuition") → future (FSP → named Bachelor → labs/internship).
  • CV: tabular, 1–2 pages, reverse-chronological, no gaps since Inter, identical spellings everywhere. LORs ×2: Inter Maths + Physics lecturers, letterhead, your rank + two specific examples.
  • Interview (5–15 min): know your city, semester fee, housing plan and course length (Kolleg 2 sem + Bachelor 6 sem); explain the gap year with certificates; know your sponsor and the €992/mo payout.
  • Landing: housing confirmation → Anmeldung within 14 days → bank activation → enrolment → residence permit (~€100, eAT card).
Chapter X

Scholarships, jobs, Blue Card, PR

  • Scholarships: Deutschlandstipendium €300/mo (all nationalities) · DAAD EPOS €992/mo (needs 2 yrs experience) · WISE research internships (IIT/NIT) · Erasmus Mundus €1,400/mo.
  • After graduation: 18-month job-seeker visa → EU Blue Card 2026: €50,700 (MINT €45,934 — every aero starting salary of €48–75k clears it: Airbus €58–68k · MTU €60–72k · DLR ~€52–60k).
  • PR in 21 months with B1 German (27 with A1); citizenship in 5 years — note India bans dual citizenship (you would take OCI).
  • Clearance: military/secret projects (Eurofighter, A400M-military, FCAS) routinely need EU/NATO citizenship — target civil programs first, disclose nationality early.
